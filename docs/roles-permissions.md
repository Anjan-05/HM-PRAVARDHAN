---

## Detailed Role Responsibilities

### 1. Public Visitor (`VISITOR`)
* **Objective**: Transparent governance for parents, citizens, and education researchers.
* **Capabilities**:
  * Search any school or Gazetted Headmaster in Telangana.
  * Browse the official rankings table filtered by District, Mandal, or Category.
  * View verified school achievements, faculty count, and infrastructure ratings.
  * Zero administrative access; no ability to alter evaluation or marks data.

### 2. Headmaster (`HEADMASTER`)
* **Objective**: Institutional administration and performance improvement.
* **Capabilities**:
  * Immediate visibility of personal ranking metrics (Current, Mandal, District, Previous, Change) upon login.
  * Category score breakdown and automated priority improvement notices.
  * Historical performance trend graph across evaluation terms (Term 1, Term 2, Term 3).
  * Direct Class 10 SSC marks entry and board pass percentage tracking.
  * Submission of verified milestones for bonus credits (up to 10 points).
  * Filing of official grievance reports for infrastructure or staffing delays.

### 3. Mandal Education Officer (`MEO`)
* **Objective**: Sub-district inspection, supervision, and verification.
* **Capabilities**:
  * Monitor all schools and Headmasters within the assigned Mandal.
  * View student pass rates and performance trajectories across the Mandal.
  * Conduct institutional inspections and recommend achievements for district approval.

### 4. District Education Officer (`DEO`)
* **Objective**: District-level apex evaluation and governance.
* **Capabilities**:
  * District-wide performance analytics and ranking distributions.
  * Official verification of achievement submissions and bonus credit allocation.
  * Resolution of school bottleneck grievances and resource sanctioning.
  * Adjudication of score appeals with authority to revise category ratings.
